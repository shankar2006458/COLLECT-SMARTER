How to collect your payment

Follow these steps in order — each one builds on the last.

  1. 1

    Download each QR code (or keep this page open)

    Tap Download PNG under a QR code to save it to your device, or simply leave this summary open on your screen. Each QR code is a separate payment — you need all of them.

  2. 2

    Share the first QR code with your payer

    Send it over WhatsApp, email, or show it in person. The payer opens any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.), taps Scan & Pay, and scans the code.

  3. 3

    Payer confirms the amount and pays

    The UPI app auto-fills your UPI ID, your name, and the exact amount shown on the QR card. The payer enters their UPI PIN to complete that payment.

  4. 4

    Repeat for every remaining QR code

    If your total was split, share each remaining QR code one by one until the full amount is collected.

  5. 5

    Verify the total you received

    Check your bank or UPI app notifications. The amounts on all QR cards should add up to the Total to collect shown on the summary. You're done once the full amount has arrived.

Important: Each QR code is valid for one payment only. Never share more than one code at a time unless you expect the payer to pay the full split total. Always confirm the amount on the payer's screen before they enter their PIN.
